## The Story

James Bond forgot his gun for the mission. *Can you imagine that?*

Being his legendary gun, you decided you should go to him yourself. Q packs you into a suitcase, but the mission has already gone sideways — Bond has been captured, and he's being held on a helicopter above the city. Nobody is coming to the rescue... except his sidearm.

Smash out of skyscraper windows, climb the twilight city balcony by balcony, and put a bullet in the bad guy's plans. You are the gun. Act like it.

## What Is It?

**Up in Arms** is a **physics platformer built entirely around recoil**. You don't have legs — every shot you fire kicks you through the air, and the tumble of your own body decides where the barrel points. Movement *is* aiming *is* shooting.

- **Tap Z** — fire. The recoil launches you; where you fly depends on where the barrel was pointing mid-tumble.
- **Hold Z** — bullet time. The world slows so you can time your shot against the spin.
- **Release** — the aimed shot, slightly stronger.

Six shells in the magazine. Ammo crates on the balconies. Gunners, rocketeers, and glass barriers between you and Bond — and a fatal drop below everything. Ten levels, one button.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
|-------|--------|
| **Z** (tap) | Fire — recoil launches you |
| **Z** (hold) | Bullet time (slow-mo aim) |
| **Z** (release from hold) | Aimed shot |
| Gamepad right trigger | Same as Z |
